Vigo Vault

A Bluetooth smart padlock app that lets you connect to, unlock, and manage your physical padlock from your phone.

About

Vigo Vault is a simple utility app for Android and iOS. It uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with your compatible smart padlock — no account required.

Contact

For support, feedback, or questions about the app, email us at fitnesssnacklock@gmail.com.

We aim to respond within a few business days.

Privacy Policy

Vigo Vault does not collect personal user data. The app operates locally on your device to connect to your padlock via Bluetooth. We do not sell, share, or store your personal information.

Optional analytics or crash reporting, if enabled, may collect anonymous technical data to help improve app stability. No data is used to identify you.
