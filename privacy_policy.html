Privacy Policy

Vigo Vault · Effective date: July 30, 2026 · Last updated: July 30, 2026

Vigo Vault is a simple Bluetooth utility app that lets you connect to and control a compatible physical padlock. The app uses a sports and fitness visual theme for appearance only. It does not track your workouts, read health data, or connect to fitness platforms such as Health Connect, Google Fit, or similar services.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Vigo Vault ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Vigo Vault mobile application (the "App") on Android and iOS devices. By using the App, you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information stored locally on your device

Most App data stays on your phone and is not uploaded to our servers. This includes:

  • Saved lock names and identifiers
  • Lock display order and primary lock preference
  • Last-known battery level and connection details for paired locks
  • App settings and preferences

This information is stored using on-device storage (such as SharedPreferences) and remains on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the App.

3.2 Bluetooth data

To connect to and control your padlock, the App uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE). This requires access to nearby Bluetooth devices. The App may process:

  • Bluetooth device identifiers (such as MAC addresses or device IDs)
  • Device names broadcast by the lock
  • Signal strength (RSSI) to help indicate proximity
  • Messages sent to and received from the lock to perform connect, unlock, and status operations

Bluetooth is used solely to operate your hardware lock. We do not use Bluetooth for advertising, unrelated location tracking, or building profiles of places you visit.

3.3 Optional diagnostic and analytics data

If enabled in the App, we may collect limited technical information to keep the App stable and improve reliability, such as:

  • Crash reports and error logs (for example, via Firebase Crashlytics)
  • Device model, operating system version, and app version
  • Basic usage or performance events related to app stability

This data is used for troubleshooting and improvement. We do not sell it, and it is not used to track your health or fitness activity.

3.4 Information you provide voluntarily

If you contact us for support, we receive the information you choose to send (such as your email address and message content) so we can respond.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

Vigo Vault does not:

  • Access Health Connect, step counts, calories, heart rate, or other health or fitness data
  • Monitor your workouts or gym activity
  • Lock or unlock other apps on your phone based on fitness goals
  • Collect precise GPS location for tracking purposes
  • Require an account to use basic lock control features

5. Device Permissions

The App may request permissions needed for its features, including:

  • Bluetooth scan and connect: to discover and communicate with your padlock (Android and iOS)
  • Internet: to send optional crash reports and load remote content if applicable
  • Notifications: for optional alerts such as low lock battery warnings

On older Android versions, legacy Bluetooth discovery may require location permission as required by the operating system. The App does not use that permission to track where you go.

You can revoke permissions at any time in your device settings, though some features may stop working.

6. How We Use Information

We use information only to:

  • Connect to and control your Bluetooth padlock
  • Save your lock list and preferences on your device
  • Display lock status such as connection state and battery level
  • Diagnose crashes and improve app reliability, where analytics are enabled
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with applicable law

We do not sell your personal information.

7. How We Share Information

We do not share your locally stored lock settings with third parties for marketing. Limited data may be processed by service providers that help us operate the App, such as:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics — crash and error reporting (Firebase Privacy)

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users and our service.

8. Data Retention

Lock settings stored on your device remain until you delete them or uninstall the App. Crash and diagnostic data retained by analytics providers is kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and service improvement, according to those providers' policies.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information processed by the App. Communication with your padlock depends on Bluetooth security and your physical device. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.
